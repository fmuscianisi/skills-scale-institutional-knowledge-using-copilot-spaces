# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a customer-first, iterative process with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, regular communication, and continuous learning.

## Project management processes

Projects begin by validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and defining goals and success measures. Approved initiatives are planned as prioritized, estimated work with acceptance criteria, milestones, dependencies, and a Definition of Done. Teams deliver in small increments, track work on a project board, review and test changes, and communicate progress, risks, and blockers. Releases require satisfied acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, release notes, a rollback plan, and smoke tests during deployment. After sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents, teams capture learnings and track owned improvement actions.
