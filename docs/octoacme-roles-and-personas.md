# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers research user needs and design usable, accessible product experiences that support the agreed outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and map user journeys
- Create and validate interaction flows, prototypes, and interface designs
- Document usability and accessibility considerations for implementation and testing

### Goals
- Make product experiences intuitive and accessible
- Validate design decisions with user evidence

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the PdM to connect user needs and design choices to product outcomes and priorities
- Coordinate with the PM on design milestones, dependencies, and stakeholder reviews
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and clarify design intent
- Collaborate with QA/Testing to define usability and accessibility checks
- Gather feedback from Stakeholders and, where appropriate, users

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and translate them into traceable, testable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Analyze current processes and document business rules and requirements
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and dependencies in proposed changes
- Connect requirements to backlog items and acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Ensure the delivered solution addresses agreed business needs
- Reduce ambiguity and requirement-related rework

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the PdM and Stakeholders to elicit, validate, and prioritize needs
- Coordinate with the PM to surface requirement dependencies and decision dates
- Clarify requirements with Developers during design and implementation
- Partner with QA/Testing to make requirements verifiable and trace tests to acceptance criteria

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide technical design and integration decisions while helping the team manage technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate technical design and document significant decisions
- Identify architecture, integration, scalability, and maintainability risks
- Support technical estimates and keep implementation aligned with agreed designs

### Goals
- Enable a coherent, secure, and maintainable solution
- Surface technical constraints and trade-offs early

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Guide Developers through design choices and implementation coordination without replacing their delivery ownership
- Share estimates, technical dependencies, and risks with the PM
- Discuss technical options and scope trade-offs with the PdM
- Work with QA/Testing to ensure the design is testable and quality risks are addressed
- Explain material technical impacts to Stakeholders with the PM and PdM

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and the activities needed to deploy changes safely and communicate their impact.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release scope, readiness checks, and deployment dependencies
- Ensure verification, release notes, and rollback or mitigation plans are prepared
- Track release decisions and communicate timing and status

### Goals
- Make releases predictable, observable, and recoverable
- Ensure teams and affected stakeholders are ready for deployment

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate release dates, dependencies, and status with the PM
- Align release scope and customer-facing messaging with the PdM
- Work with Developers on deployment steps and rollback plans
- Coordinate pre- and post-deployment verification with QA/Testing
- Inform Stakeholders about release timing, impact, and readiness

---

## Operations / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Operations or Support Representatives bring production operations, service support, and customer-impact perspectives into planning and delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify supportability, operational readiness, and service-impact requirements
- Contribute to monitoring, runbook, escalation, and handover planning
- Share recurring customer issues and operational feedback with the delivery team

### Goals
- Make delivered changes supportable and observable
- Reduce avoidable disruption and improve response to customer-impacting issues

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the PM to record operational risks, dependencies, and readiness actions
- Partner with the PdM to connect customer feedback and service impact to product priorities
- Coordinate with Developers on observability, runbooks, and operational handover
- Work with QA/Testing to include operational and failure scenarios in verification
- Communicate support readiness and emerging issues to Stakeholders

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Teams may combine roles on smaller projects; the responsibilities and handoffs should still have clear owners.
